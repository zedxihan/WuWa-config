# Recommended In-Game Graphics Settings

**GPU:** Intel Arc B580

**Graphics Quality:** UHD in 1080p (FHD)

**Performance:** 100–150 FPS with 2x FG + RT Low

**Notes:**
- Tweak the settings based on your hardware and personal preference. Recommended to keep below the 'Laggy' (RED) indicator.
- Make sure Auto FPS is turned off to avoid performance issues regarding GPU-utilization.
- Use lower Render Distance and Foliage Density/LOD in **1440p** or if you experience performance issues.
- XeSS3 + 2x FG + RT Low is the recommended combination for Arc B580.
